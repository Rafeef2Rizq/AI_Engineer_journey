FAQs

Is Billow really free invoicing software?+

Yes. There's no subscription. You pay a small fee per payment, only when a client pays you.

How does Billow compare to other invoicing tools on cost?+

There are no seats or tiers, and card rates start at 2.7%. Bank and stablecoin payments cost 1%.

Will my clients pay an invoice from a tool they haven't used before?+

Yes. They don't need an account. They open the link and pay with the method they already use.

How does the payment link work?+

Each invoice gets a secure link that shows every payment method you've enabled.

What payment methods can my clients use?+

Cards, bank transfers (ACH and SEPA), popular wallets and stablecoins.

How fast do I get paid?+

Most payments settle the same day.

Does Billow work with my accounting software?+

Who is Billow built for?+
